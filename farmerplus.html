The ecosystem / FarmerPlus

Practical support.
Closer to the farmer.

FarmerPlus is the farmer-support and agricultural operations layer within TerraNexus. It connects the people, information and services around day-to-day agricultural activity.

Its role in one sentence

Support the farmer, understand the activity, and coordinate the help needed to move forward.

The farmer journey

Help that follows the farmer’s needs.

01

Onboarding & participation

Bring farmers into the programme through the appropriate cooperative and field relationships, with relevant participant information.

02

Learning & development

Connect farmers and local teams to practical learning that supports their roles and the selected value chain.

03

Field support

Coordinate local advice, service follow-up and practical assistance around real production activity.

04

Verification & farm data

Build an evidence base around the farmer, the farm and the activity that an implementation needs to understand.

From activity to opportunity

Connect the work beyond the field.

  • Coordinate services and input support around identified needs.
  • Connect production with buyer requirements and market opportunities.
  • Support the information needed for aggregation, quality checks and delivery.
  • Capture field activity and impact evidence that can inform programme decisions.

The scope depends on the implementation. A pilot should specify the crop or value chain, farmer cohort, field team, services and evidence requirements before activity begins.

Separate and complementary

FarmerPlus supports the activity.
FORUS-Grow supports the infrastructure.

FarmerPlus and FORUS-Grow responsibilities
FarmerPlus FORUS-Grow
Farmer onboarding and field engagement Cooperative infrastructure and digital participation
Service coordination and delivery evidence Merchant, supplier and controlled-funding workflows
Farm data, verification and impact evidence Settlement, reconciliation and economic reporting

A verified agricultural activity can inform a payment or reporting process. The two layers work together without becoming the same product.
