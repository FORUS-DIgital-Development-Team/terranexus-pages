The ecosystem / FORUS-Grow

The infrastructure
around agricultural activity.

FORUS-Grow is the FORUS agriculture-focused digital, economic and financial operating layer. It connects cooperative participation with funding, transactions and accountable economic workflows.

Built on the FORUS foundation

Shared identity, connected platforms, transactions and integration support the agricultural economy around the farmer.

Accountability

Know where value is meant to go.

An agricultural programme may involve a funder, cooperative, farmer, supplier, service provider and buyer. FORUS-Grow provides the economic operating layer around those relationships.

Controlled allocation and reporting can connect funding decisions to programme activity. Settlement and reconciliation provide the record of how that value moved, subject to the approved implementation and financial providers.

Working together

A practical division of responsibility.

FarmerPlus supports farmer onboarding, learning, field support, verification, farm data and service coordination. FORUS-Grow supports cooperative infrastructure, wallets, controlled funding, supplier participation, settlement and reporting.

For example, agricultural delivery evidence from the farmer-support layer can inform an agreed release or settlement condition in the economic layer. The programme defines the evidence, decision-maker and exceptions.

Responsible implementation

Scope the capability to the programme.

The FORUS platform catalogue contains capabilities at different stages of readiness. A product-family label is not a promise that every related function is generally available.

Each TerraNexus implementation needs an agreed market, operating scope and provider setup. Payments and regulated financial services depend on the appropriate providers and approvals.
