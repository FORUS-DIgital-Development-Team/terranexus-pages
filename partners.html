The ecosystem / Partners

Shared purpose.
Distinct contributions.

The ecosystem depends on organisations that bring agricultural reach, local trust, practical delivery and enabling infrastructure. Understanding each role makes the whole model clearer.

TerraNexus at the centre

A common agricultural purpose connects the participants. Each organisation keeps its own role and identity.

Institutional and enabling roles

The connections behind the ecosystem.

Agricultural reach

World Agriculture Forum

WAF contributes global agricultural relationships and country-level engagement. Its Country Councils are intended to connect local priorities with national and international stakeholders. The FORUS–WAF collaboration supporting TerraNexus was announced on 10 September 2026.

Learn more
Cooperative participation

FORUS Digital Cooperative

FORUS Digital Cooperative operates the cooperative ecosystem at FORUS.coop. It connects cooperative participation with the shared FORUS infrastructure and supports the cooperative-first approach to TerraNexus.

Learn more
Purpose & governance

The Dream

The Dream provides the wider purpose of connecting people, organisations, technology and opportunity. The TerraNexus presentation assigns it a role in member vetting, contracting, governance and protecting the integrity of the ecosystem.

Learn more
Cooperative identity

.coop / DotCooperation

The .coop domain provides a recognisable digital identity for eligible cooperatives. FORUS’s partnership with DotCooperation supports awareness, onboarding and practical adoption across Africa.

Learn more

Practical delivery

The field is where the model becomes real.

The presentation’s field-delivery context connects learning, production and aggregation.

Learning & field support

U Can Grow

Presented as a practical agricultural-development platform, U Can Grow combines low-cost regenerative farming education, field support and connections to contract-farming opportunities. It provides part of the proof-of-concept context in the presentation.

Learn more
Aggregation context

agritec earth

The presentation places agritec earth alongside U Can Grow in the aggregation and proof-of-concept context. Its detailed responsibilities should be defined within the relevant field-delivery arrangement.

Learn more

Wider alignment

A place for additional capability.

TerraNexus is intended to connect other agricultural cooperatives, technology and innovation providers, suppliers, buyers, logistics partners, institutions and funders. Participation depends on the needs and arrangements of each implementation.

The presentation also discusses the Global Flagship Initiative for Food Security and references AgriConnect, AGROW Nigeria and CAADP. References to these initiatives describe strategic context and potential alignment; they do not by themselves establish a TerraNexus partnership or funding commitment.
