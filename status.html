TerraNexus combines an emerging agricultural ecosystem, established field activity and FORUS capabilities at different stages of readiness. This page makes those distinctions explicit.

Implementation context

Different parts, different stages.

Implementation context by area
Area Published context
TerraNexus The FORUS–WAF collaboration was announced on 10 September 2026. TerraNexus is described as in development, with priority areas for implementation to be defined.
FORUS.coop The public cooperative ecosystem is operating. Individual capabilities remain subject to their own readiness, market and deployment requirements.
FarmerPlus & FORUS-Grow Separate and complementary roles in the TerraNexus model. A pilot must define the capabilities and workflows actually included.
Agri-Dollar The presentation describes purpose-bound funding and settlement models. Programme rules, providers and operating arrangements are implementation-specific.
Field material The supplied presentation provides proof-of-concept descriptions and programme photographs. These do not establish audited results for the whole TerraNexus ecosystem.

Reading the programme information

Capability is scoped to the implementation.

Descriptions of a platform’s role explain what it is intended to support. They are not a statement that every capability is active in every country or programme. Financial services depend on appropriate providers and approvals.

References to potential collaborators and wider agricultural initiatives do not establish signed agreements, available funding or guaranteed outcomes. Contact TerraNexus for the scope of a specific opportunity.
