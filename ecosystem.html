Your questions

A few useful distinctions.

Is TerraNexus another name for FORUS?

No. TerraNexus is the agricultural ecosystem. FORUS provides underlying digital and economic infrastructure that can also serve other sectors and organisations.

Are FarmerPlus and FORUS-Grow the same platform?

No. FarmerPlus supports farmers and agricultural operations. FORUS-Grow supplies the surrounding digital, economic and financial operating layer. They have separate, complementary responsibilities.

Does joining mean giving up local cooperative identity?

The cooperative-first approach is designed to preserve local identity, governance and member relationships. Participation and service activation are agreed within the relevant implementation.

Is every capability available now?

No. The FORUS catalogue distinguishes ready, pilot and planned capabilities, and availability also depends on the market, providers and implementation scope. A TerraNexus pilot defines which capabilities are actually included.
