The connected journey

A clearer path from participation to payment.

  1. 01

    Join through a cooperative

    A cooperative and its representatives enter the agreed participation process. Identity, membership and local responsibilities provide the starting point.

  2. 02

    Understand the farmer and the farm

    FarmerPlus supports onboarding, learning, field engagement and relevant farm information so the farmer’s needs and activity are understood.

  3. 03

    Coordinate the support

    Connect the right inputs, services, training and market opportunities. FORUS-Grow supports the surrounding digital and economic workflows.

  4. 04

    Deliver and verify

    Record what was provided or produced, by whom and when. Agree in advance what evidence is needed for a delivery, quality check or milestone.

  5. 05

    Settle and account

    Use the approved financial arrangements to allocate value, pay the relevant participants and reconcile what happened. Agri-Dollar can support purpose-bound programme funding.

  6. 06

    Learn and improve

    Combine agricultural evidence with delivery and settlement records to understand outcomes and improve the next cycle.

Illustrative programme

Inputs that reach their intended purpose.

A farmer voucher programme

A funder supports a defined farmer cohort.

FarmerPlus helps establish farmer participation and the agricultural need. Approved suppliers deliver eligible inputs. Delivery evidence supports the agreed payment conditions. FORUS-Grow provides the controlled allocation, settlement and reconciliation around the programme.

Agri-Dollar connects that allocation to a defined agricultural purpose, with programme rules for redemption, expiry and unused value.

Clear accountability

Every handoff needs an owner.

A connected platform does not remove the need for clear responsibilities. Each pilot identifies who onboards participants, provides support, verifies delivery, authorises payments and resolves exceptions.

The same approach applies to buyer-funded produce, a mechanisation programme or support for a regenerative transition. The activities differ; the need for traceable decisions remains.
