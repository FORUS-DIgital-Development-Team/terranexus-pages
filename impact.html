What evidence can show

Make activity useful to the next participant.

  1. 01

    Farmer and farm visibility

    Participant records and mapped agricultural activity help a programme understand who is involved and where work happens.

  2. 02

    Delivery and quality

    Field records, produce checks and service evidence help establish what was delivered and whether requirements were met.

  3. 03

    Market and financial activity

    Where included, fulfilment, settlement and reconciliation records connect the physical activity to its economic outcome.

  4. 04

    Impact over time

    Agreed indicators and baselines help distinguish participation, delivery outputs and longer-term farmer outcomes.

The evidence boundary

A practical base for the next stage.

The presentation describes a local proof of concept tested under limited connectivity and unreliable power, and includes farm mapping and evidence intended to support traceability requirements. Those are programme descriptions from the supplied material.

This site does not turn those descriptions into audited impact totals, regulatory certifications or a claim that the complete TerraNexus ecosystem is already deployed at scale. Pilot reporting should identify the location, period, method and source for each result.
